Pint's Durability - Changelog

v1.0
- First release.
- A flashing warning when your most-worn gear drops to 50%, to 25%, and when something breaks: the screen edges glow
  (yellow, orange, red), a big warning names the item, and the raid-warning sound plays. Each warning shows once;
  repairing sets them up again.
- Logging in with worn gear gives a quiet note in chat.
- Settings window (/pdur): warnings on/off, screen flash, big text, sound, login reminder, and a Test button.
  Also in Esc > Options > AddOns, and in the add-on menu on WoW Forever's minimap.
- Option: warn for each piece of gear separately. Each item gets its own 50% / 25% / broken warnings; items
  reaching a step together share one warning; swapping an item starts that slot fresh.
- /pdur status shows your most-worn item and average; /pdur test shows the three warnings; /pdur on / off;
  /pdur each; /pdur sound, /pdur flash, /pdur text switch each part.
