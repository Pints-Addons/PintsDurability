Pint's Durability v1.0 - a warning before your gear breaks
(WoW Classic Era / Hardcore / Forever)

REQUIRES
  Nothing. Standalone.

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\PintsDurability
  (or _forever_ for WoW Forever). Restart the game.

WHAT IT DOES
  Watches your most-worn piece of gear. When it drops to 50%, to 25%, and when something breaks:
  - the screen edges glow - yellow, orange, then red;
  - a big warning names the item ("Gear down to 25% - [Robe of the Moccasin] ... repair soon");
  - the raid-warning sound plays.
  Each warning shows once. Repairing sets them up again. Logging in with worn gear gives a quiet note in chat.

  Option "Warn for each piece of gear separately": every item gets its own 50% / 25% / broken warnings, so a
  second item wearing down warns too. Pieces that reach a step together share one warning. Swapping in another
  item starts that slot fresh.

SETTINGS
  /pdur, Esc > Options > AddOns > Pint's Durability, or (WoW Forever) the add-on menu on the minimap.

COMMANDS
  /pdur          settings window (each warning part, per-item option, test button)
  /pdur status   your most-worn item and the average
  /pdur each     per-item warnings on or off
  /pdur test     see the three warnings
  /pdur on|off   all warnings on or off
  /pdur sound | flash | text   switch each part on or off
